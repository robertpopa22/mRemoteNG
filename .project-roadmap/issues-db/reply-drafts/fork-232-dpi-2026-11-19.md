Thank you for the full-screen screenshot and for confirming that Options now opens at an acceptable speed. Your screenshot showed the icons in the Options navigation painting over the first letters of their labels. The renderer was drawing those icons wider than the space reserved for them. It now uses the same width in pixels for both drawing and the label offset.

In the automated Windows lab at 200% scaling, the first opening showed overlap before the change. After the change, the icons and labels occupied separate columns on both the first opening and reopening. The Connections page also passed its resize check. The app recorded 191 ms on first opening and 42 ms on reopening in that lab run.

The x64 ZIP and MSI in [nightly 20261119, commit `77a7e5f26`](https://github.com/robertpopa22/mRemoteNG/releases/tag/nightly) contain the correction. Please check the first Options opening on your 4K screen at 200% and let us know whether its labels are clear and whether the main-window layout still needs a resize. The lab used an English desktop. If either symptom persists, we will take it to human review before scheduling another automated fix.
