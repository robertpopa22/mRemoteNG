# #182: new retention evidence and incorrect measurement verdict

Source: [vndnua's September 29 report](https://github.com/robertpopa22/mRemoteNG/issues/182#issuecomment-5898279242), reviewed September 30.
This is a different reporter from Buster-de, whose September 13 measurements confirmed improvement
in their environment. It does not establish when the newly reported behavior began.

## Verified findings

- Reported baseline: 99 MB. First settled close: 274 MB (+175 MB). Fourteenth settled close:
  1,274 MB (+1,175 MB). After the first close, another 1,000 MB remains over 13 further sessions,
  approximately 76.9 MB/session. The series has intermittent decreases, but no final plateau.
- Reporter used several Windows Server 2008-2025 targets and waited 30 seconds after each close.
  The first-session peak was 431 MB, or 332 MB above baseline.
- The script printed `No accumulation` because its 77 MB average was below half of 332 MB
  (166 MB). Replaying the exact series through the old summary reproduced this false reassurance.
- Absence of `host dispose threw` only excludes that reported error message. It does not prove
  all native/managed allocations were released. Private bytes show retained process allocation,
  but do not identify its ownership or prove an unreachable allocation leak.
- The existing three-cycle xrdp acceptance test in `RdpSessionMemoryAcceptanceTests.cs` uses the
  same half-session-cost threshold. Its green result cannot refute this Windows-target report.
  Do not replace that threshold with another arbitrary cutoff and present it as a memory fix.

## Bounded correction and verification

`scripts/measure-rdp-memory.ps1` now reports the cumulative post-first-close growth, the full
per-close series and the uncertainty. It no longer prints either a definitive leak diagnosis or
a leak-free verdict from this ratio. Exit 0 means the measurement completed; fewer than three
marked closes return 2, consistently with the stated minimum.

Summary-only replay, without opening the application: the supplied 14-close series reports
`+1000 MB across 13 further sessions`; flat and decreasing series report their exact net change
without claiming leak freedom; two closes return insufficient data. All four cases passed.
This validates the reporting correction, not memory release in the product. No product code changed.
